Does arriving first make the Doorway irrelevant?No.Even the first player through loses 3.1 discs on average in 4p (tower 11.8 vs a tile of 9.1); the last loses 6.6. 85% of all arrivals leave something behind, so choosing the stretch matters for everyone.HEADLINE
Does any strategy dominate?No archetype does.The best 4p archetype (Late bloomer) wins 30.3% [29.2%, 31.6%] against a 35% ceiling; the weakest wins 21.2%. The lookahead search bot wins 36.1%, just over the ceiling — skilful play is rewarded, mostly by Rushing more (4.4 per game) and arriving earlier.BALANCE
Is there a seat advantage?Small.The top of the starting stack wins 25.2% in 4p vs a fair 25.0% (+0.2 pts). Worst seat gap by player count: 2p 0.1 pts, 3p 0.6 pts, 4p 0.2 pts.SEATS
Is Rush worth it?Rarely, as the bots play it: 13.6% of 4p moves are Rushes.Players who never rush win 28.4% (fair 25.0%); players who rush 3+ times win 16.2%.TEMPO

Suggested rule tweaks

Each cites its sweep. Nothing changes until you accept it in the sandbox.

  1. Try 4-player Doorway tiles 10/8/6/4 — Arriving first starts to matter more: the gap between first and last arrival grows to 6.91 discs (from 4.02) while the strongest bot does not gain. Scores drop by about 2.5 points, so the scoring scale would need a look.
    doorway.4 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 10/8/6/4: top strategy set_collector 28.5%, spread 10.5%, non-first arrivals lose 7.00 (1st 2.35, last 9.26), Full life 3.5% of points, avg score 26.6; baseline 9/8/7/6: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1. S4C (lookahead in every game, 2,000 games): lookahead wins 30.9% vs 32.3% at baseline.
  2. Try 3-player tiles 10/7/5 — Strategies end closer together (spread 13.9% vs 16.4%) and arriving first matters more (gap 5.96 vs 2.95 discs).
    doorway.3 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 10/7/5: top strategy set_collector 38.7%, spread 13.9%, non-first arrivals lose 7.80 (1st 3.02, last 8.98), Full life 4.2% of points, avg score 27.7; baseline 9/8/7: top strategy set_collector 40.6%, spread 16.4%, non-first arrivals lose 6.17 (1st 3.85, last 6.80), Full life 5.4% of points, avg score 30.7.
  3. Try 2-player tiles 9/6 — Strategies end closer together (spread 15.4% vs 23.6%) and arriving first matters more (gap 3.54 vs 2.62 discs).
    doorway.2 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 9/6: top strategy passion_specialist 55.1%, spread 15.4%, non-first arrivals lose 7.54 (1st 4.00, last 7.54), Full life 5.7% of points, avg score 28.0; baseline 9/7: top strategy late_bloomer 58.5%, spread 23.6%, non-first arrivals lose 6.52 (1st 3.90, last 6.52), Full life 6.4% of points, avg score 29.9.
  4. Raise the Full life bonus to 8 — Sets are only 4.5% of all points today; at 8 they become 6.7% without widening the gap between strategies, so the generalist path matters more.
    fullLifeBonus sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 8: top strategy set_collector 29.9%, spread 9.7%, non-first arrivals lose 5.95 (1st 3.15, last 7.17), Full life 6.7% of points, avg score 29.5; baseline 6: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1.
  5. Drop the experience cap from the rules (or keep it only as a safety rail) — With no cap the game plays the same (top win 29.5% vs 29.4%, same discs lost): towers rarely reach 8 discs of one domain, so the −4 cap almost never binds.
    experienceCap sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 99: top strategy passion_specialist 29.5%, spread 9.5%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.18), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1; baseline 4: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1. S4C (lookahead in every game, 2,000 games): lookahead wins 31.4% vs 31.4% at baseline.
