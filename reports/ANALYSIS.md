## 11. Suggested rule tweaks

Suggestions only — the default ruleset is unchanged. Each cites the sweep row that backs it; try them in `/designer` → Sandbox.

1. **Try 4-player Doorway tiles 10/8/6/4.** Arriving first starts to matter more: the gap between first and last arrival grows to 6.91 discs (from 4.02) while the strongest bot does not gain. Scores drop by about 2.5 points, so the scoring scale would need a look.
   _Evidence:_ doorway.4 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 10/8/6/4: top strategy set_collector 28.5%, spread 10.5%, non-first arrivals lose 7.00 (1st 2.35, last 9.26), Full life 3.5% of points, avg score 26.6; baseline 9/8/7/6: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1. S4C (lookahead in every game, 2,000 games): lookahead wins 30.9% vs 32.3% at baseline.
2. **Try 3-player tiles 10/7/5.** Strategies end closer together (spread 13.9% vs 16.4%) and arriving first matters more (gap 5.96 vs 2.95 discs).
   _Evidence:_ doorway.3 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 10/7/5: top strategy set_collector 38.7%, spread 13.9%, non-first arrivals lose 7.80 (1st 3.02, last 8.98), Full life 4.2% of points, avg score 27.7; baseline 9/8/7: top strategy set_collector 40.6%, spread 16.4%, non-first arrivals lose 6.17 (1st 3.85, last 6.80), Full life 5.4% of points, avg score 30.7.
3. **Try 2-player tiles 9/6.** Strategies end closer together (spread 15.4% vs 23.6%) and arriving first matters more (gap 3.54 vs 2.62 discs).
   _Evidence:_ doorway.2 sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 9/6: top strategy passion_specialist 55.1%, spread 15.4%, non-first arrivals lose 7.54 (1st 4.00, last 7.54), Full life 5.7% of points, avg score 28.0; baseline 9/7: top strategy late_bloomer 58.5%, spread 23.6%, non-first arrivals lose 6.52 (1st 3.90, last 6.52), Full life 6.4% of points, avg score 29.9.
4. **Raise the Full life bonus to 8.** Sets are only 4.5% of all points today; at 8 they become 6.7% without widening the gap between strategies, so the generalist path matters more.
   _Evidence:_ fullLifeBonus sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 8: top strategy set_collector 29.9%, spread 9.7%, non-first arrivals lose 5.95 (1st 3.15, last 7.17), Full life 6.7% of points, avg score 29.5; baseline 6: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1.
5. **Drop the experience cap from the rules (or keep it only as a safety rail).** With no cap the game plays the same (top win 29.5% vs 29.4%, same discs lost): towers rarely reach 8 discs of one domain, so the −4 cap almost never binds.
   _Evidence:_ experienceCap sweep (S4, 10,000 games per value) — 99: top strategy passion_specialist 29.5%, spread 9.5%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.18), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1; baseline 4: top strategy passion_specialist 29.4%, spread 9.4%, non-first arrivals lose 5.97 (1st 3.17, last 7.19), Full life 4.5% of points, avg score 29.1. S4C (lookahead in every game, 2,000 games): lookahead wins 31.4% vs 31.4% at baseline.

## Method notes

- Bots: `packages/bots/strategies.json`. Weighted scorers use the features in `packages/bots/src/features.ts`. `lookahead` is determinized Monte Carlo (16 determinizations × ≤ 6 candidate moves, greedy rollouts; S4 field uses 4 × 5).
- Every bot picks its Doorway window optimally (spec §6), so discs lost here are the minimum possible for each tower.
- Seeds are fixed (`doorway-2026-*`); rerunning `pnpm sim:study` reproduces every number. Raw runs live in `reports/runs/` (gitignored); `reports/data/` holds the aggregated CSVs, Parquet copies and a 1,000-game move-log sample.
- Win rates count a k-way shared win as 1/k (R28).
