How to play, in five breaths

You'll pick up the rest as you go.

  1. One life, 40 years. Your pawn walks a path from Young to Old. At the end is the Doorway.
  2. Quest. Spend years on a card, move that far, and stack its memory disc on top of your tower.
  3. Furthest back goes next. No rounds. Cheap quests can earn you several turns in a row.
  4. →| Or rush. Jump just past the nearest pawn ahead. No memory, but you're closer to the Doorway.
  5. Keep one stretch. Arrive early and you carry more (9 · 8 · 7 · 6). Points come only from the unbroken stretch you keep.
